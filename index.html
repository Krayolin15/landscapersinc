About Landscapers Inc.

We don't just maintain landscapes. We transform them.

Landscapers Inc. is a professional landscaping company based in Mount Edgecombe, Durban. We specialise in transforming and maintaining outdoor environments through thoughtful garden design, professional maintenance and quality workmanship.

From residential landscapes to larger properties, our focus is to deliver outdoor spaces that are functional, attractive and carefully maintained.

  • Professional landscaping team
  • Custom-designed solutions
  • Scheduled garden maintenance
  • Eco-conscious practices
  • Free site assessments
  • Mount Edgecombe, Umhlanga, Durban & surrounds
